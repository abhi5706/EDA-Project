# SwiftCart Business Insights Report

1. **Customers:** Women have the larger record share, and Adults are the largest age group.
2. **Products:** `Set` is the leading category by sales.
3. **Channels:** `Amazon` is the leading channel by sales.
4. **Geography:** `MAHARASHTRA`, `KARNATAKA`, and `UTTAR PRADESH` are the top three states by sales.
5. **Time:** `Mar` has the highest observed monthly sales; `Nov` is the lowest.
6. **Orders:** Delivered is the dominant status; returned, cancelled, and refunded orders remain operationally relevant.
7. **Data quality:** Missing demographic and date fields remain, so affected analyses should state the limitation.
8. **Statistics:** Selected tests provide evidence about association/differences, not causal proof.
