# SwiftCart Final Business Recommendations

1. **Strengthen leading categories:** Prioritize inventory and assortment review for `Set` and other high-sales categories.
2. **Prioritize high-performing channels:** Review campaigns and assortment on `Amazon` while comparing order outcomes across channels.
3. **Focus regional planning:** Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh are priority markets by observed sales.
4. **Monitor order outcomes:** Compare returns, cancellations, and refunds by channel and category to identify operational areas needing attention.
5. **Plan around monthly variation:** Use observed monthly patterns for inventory and campaign planning, while recognizing the one-year observation window and missing dates.
6. **Use engineered customer/order features:** Customer order count, order value, and basket measures can support repeat-purchase and customer-engagement analysis.
7. **Maintain data quality controls:** Validate IDs, ages, dates, categories, and whitespace at source to reduce downstream cleaning.

All recommendations are limited to findings supported by the available dataset.
